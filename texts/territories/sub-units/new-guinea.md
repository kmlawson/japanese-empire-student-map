# The two territories

## Papua

Australian territory outright, not a mandate. Japan never took Port Moresby: the seaborne attempt turned back at the Coral Sea and the overland push was stopped on the Kokoda Track in September 1942.

## NewGuineaMandate

The former German colony, held by Australia under a League mandate and administered from Rabaul — which Japan took in January 1942 and made the base for the whole southern campaign.
