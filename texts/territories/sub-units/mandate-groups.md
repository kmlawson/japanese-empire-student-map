# The groups

## Marianas

The chain Japan took from Germany in 1914, less Guam. Its fall in the summer of 1944 put Japan within B-29 range and brought down the Tōjō cabinet.

## Palau

The seat of the South Seas Bureau at Koror from 1922, and the westernmost of the mandate. Peleliu and Angaur were taken in 1944; the rest was bypassed.

## Yap

A cable and radio station, and the subject of a long dispute between Japan and the United States in the 1920s. Bypassed and bombed.

## Chuuk

Truk lagoon, the Combined Fleet's central Pacific anchorage — the "Gibraltar of the Pacific" until the carrier raids of February 1944 showed it was nothing of the kind.

## Pohnpei

Ponape, the wettest and most fertile of the Carolines, with a Japanese agricultural station. Bypassed and bombed.

## Kosrae

The easternmost of the Carolines, with the basalt ruins of Lelu on its shore. Bypassed entirely and never assaulted.

## Marshalls

The outermost ring of the mandate, and the first of it to go: Kwajalein and Majuro fell in January 1944 and Enewetak in February.
