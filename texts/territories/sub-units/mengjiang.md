> The three governments Mengchiang was federated out of, as the 1940 sheet draws
> them. Two were Chinese-populated administrations under Japanese officers —
> Chanan out of southern Chahar, Jinbei out of northern Shanxi — and the third
> is the Mongol leagues themselves, which is what the state was nominally for.
> Zhangjiakou was Chanan's seat and is drawn inside the Mongol block on this sheet;
> it was also where the federation as a whole was governed from.

## The Mongol leagues

The Ulanchab and Silingol leagues on the plateau north of the Wall — grazing
land, Mongol banners, and the constituency for the autonomy Prince
Demchugdongrub was offered and Japan supervised.

## North Shansi (Jinbei) Administration

The loess uplands of northern Shansi around Tatung, coal country — the mines
worked hard through the occupation, and the ground the Eighth Route Army
contested from the hills.

## South Chahar (Chanan) Administration

The Chahar basin around Kalgan, the old gate on the caravan road to Urga; the
administrative seat of the federation and the junction of its railways.
