> The Ellice Islands, one entry per atoll. The names are the ones in use then
> and now; the colony's own spelling of Nanumanga was Nanumaga.

## Nanumea

An American airfield was built here in 1943, one of three in the Ellice from which the Gilberts were attacked.

# Atoll by atoll

## Nanumanga

Never occupied by either side. A wartime American landing party is remembered chiefly for what it did to the reef.

## Niutao

Never occupied. The Ellice were the nearest unoccupied ground to the Japanese perimeter.

## Nui

Never occupied.

## Vaitupu

The most populous of the Ellice, and the site of the colony's secondary school.

## Nukufetau

An American airfield from 1943, the second of the three built in the group.

## Nukulaelae

Never occupied. Two thirds of its people had been carried off by Peruvian slavers in 1863 and it never recovered the numbers.
