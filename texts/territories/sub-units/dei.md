> the Netherlands Indies. Java had three gouvernements from 1930 — West Java
> from 1926, East Java from 1929 — with Jogjakarta and Soerakarta left under
> their own rulers; Borneo was divided west from south-east, and the rest of
> the Outer Possessions were residencies answering to Batavia.

## Noord Banjoemas

The northern part of the Banjoemas country in south-western Central Java. By
the later 1930s Banjoemas was one residency, seated at Banjoemas, with the
regencies of Banjoemas, Poerwokerto, Tjilatjap, Karanganjar and Bandjarnegara.

## Pekalongan

A residency on the north coast of Central Java, its seat the port of
Pekalongan, with the regencies of Pekalongan, Batang, Pemalang, Tegal and
Brebes.

## Wonosobo

A division of the Central Javanese highlands. By the later 1930s Wonosobo was
a regency of the Kedoe residency.

## Semarang

The residency round the port of Semarang, which was also the seat of the
Midden-Java province. Its regencies were Semarang, Kendal, Demak and Grobogan.

## Blora

A division of inland Central Java. By the later 1930s Blora was a regency of
the Japara-Rembang residency, which was seated at Pati.

## Koedoes

A residency of Central Java between 1928 and 1931, seated at Koedoes and
taking in the Demak and Jepara country as well. After 1931 Koedoes was a
regency of the Japara-Rembang residency.

## Rembang

A division of the north-east coast of Central Java. By the later 1930s Rembang
was a regency of the Japara-Rembang residency, seated at Pati.

## Kedoe

A residency of Central Java covering the Kebumen, Wonosobo, Temanggung,
Purworejo and Magelang country, with Banjoemas to the west of it and Semarang
to the north.

## Bagelen

A division of southern Central Java round Poerworedjo, within the Kedoe
residency.

## Soerakarta

One of the two Vorstenlanden of Central Java, left under its own ruler instead
of being made a residency. When Java and Madoera were divided into three
provinces in 1925, Soerakarta and Jogjakarta were set beside them as
gouvernements, each seated in the town of its own name.

## Klaten

A division of the Soerakarta lands of Central Java, in the country between
Soerakarta and Jogjakarta.

## Jogjakarta

The other of the two Vorstenlanden, a Javanese sultanate in being since 1755,
left under its sultan as a gouvernement of the Indies rather than made a
residency. Much of its territory had been annexed after the Java War of
1825–30.

## Bodjonegoro

A division of East Java on the lower Solo river, inland from the north coast.

## Grisee

Also Gresik. A division of East Java on the north coast immediately west of
Soerabaja.

## Soerabaja

The division round Soerabaja, the second city of the Indies and the seat of
the Oost-Java province, on the strait between Java and Madoera.

## Madioen

A division of inland East Java on the Madioen river, west of Kediri.

## Kediri

A division of inland East Java on the Brantas.

## Modjokerto

A division of East Java on the Brantas inland from Soerabaja.

## Pasoeroean

A division of the north-east coast of East Java, between Soerabaja and
Probolinggo.

## Ponorogo

A division of inland East Java south of Madioen.

## Blitar

A division of southern East Java on the upper Brantas.

## Malang

A division of East Java in the highlands south of Pasoeroean, round the hill
town of Malang.

## Probolinggo

Also Prabalingga. A division of the north-east coast of East Java, east of
Pasoeroean.

## Bondowoso

A division of the Besoeki country in the eastern corner of Java.

## Djember

A division of the Besoeki country in the south-eastern corner of Java.

## West-Madoera

One of the two residencies the island of Madoera was divided into between 1928
and 1931. Before and after those years it was a single residency, seated at
Pamekasan.

## Oost-Madoera

The other of the two residencies Madoera was divided into between 1928 and
1931, the island being one residency before and after.

## Bantam

The westernmost residency of Java, in the West-Java province, seated at Serang
and made up of the regencies of Serang, Lebak and Pandeglang.

## Batavia

The residency round the capital of the Indies, on the north coast of Java.
Batavia was the seat of the West-Java province as well, and by the later 1930s
the residency’s regencies were Batavia, Meester-Cornelis and Krawang.

## Buitenzorg

A residency of West Java in the hills behind Batavia, in being from 1817 to
1867 and again from 1925 to 1942. Its seat, Buitenzorg, was also where the
colonial government sat after 1905; its regencies were Buitenzorg, Soekaboemi
and Tjiandjoer.

## West-Priangan

Also Parahyangan. Successor to the Preanger Regencies Residency. One of the
three residencies the Preanger Regencies were divided into when the provinces
were made in 1925, in the Parahyangan highlands of West Java. The three were
put back together as the Priangan residency after 1931, seated at Bandoeng.

## Midden-Priangan

Also Parahyangan. Successor to the Preanger Regencies Residency. The middle of
the three residencies the Preanger Regencies were divided into in 1925, in the
Parahyangan highlands of West Java. Bandoeng, the old and later seat of the
whole, stood in it.

## Krawang

A division of West Java on the coast east of Batavia. By the later 1930s it
was a regency of the Batavia residency, seated at Poerwakarta.

## Indramajoe

A division of the north coast of West Java between Krawang and Cheribon. By
the later 1930s it was a regency of the Cheribon residency.

## Oost-Priangan

Also Parahyangan. Successor to the Preanger Regencies Residency. The eastern
of the three residencies the Preanger Regencies were divided into in 1925, the
Parahyangan highlands running towards Cheribon. The three were reunited as
Priangan after 1931.

## Cheribon

A residency on the north coast of West Java from 1818 to 1942, its seat the
port of Cheribon. Its regencies were Cheribon, Koeningan, Indramajoe and
Madjalengka.

## Tegal

A division of the north coast of Central Java. By the later 1930s Tegal was a
regency of the Pekalongan residency.

## Zuid Banjoemas

The southern part of the Banjoemas country in south-western Central Java,
reaching the Indian Ocean at Tjilatjap. By the later 1930s the whole was one
residency seated at Banjoemas.

## Atjeh en Onderhoorigheden

The northern tip of Sumatra, a gouvernement from the late nineteenth century
with its capital at Koetaradja. The reorganisation of 1938 took away its
governor and made it a residency.

## Djambi

A residency of eastern Sumatra, established in 1906.

## Palembang

The division of south-eastern Sumatra. The sultanate of Palembang, proclaimed
in 1659, was dissolved by the colonial government in 1823 and its last ruler
exiled to Banda Neira.

## Tapanoeli

A residency of northern Sumatra with its capital at Sibolga, in being in one
form or another from 1844 to the end of Dutch rule. It covered much of the
Batak country and took in Lake Toba.

## Oostkust van Sumatra

The residency of north-eastern Sumatra, its capital Medan. In 1938 the
northern part of it, the Estates Area, held 10,026 square kilometres of
plantations.

## Riouw en Onderhoorigheden

A residency of the Indies covering the islands and coast between eastern
Sumatra and Singapore, the southern half of what is now Riau.

## Bangka en Onderhoorigheden

The division of Bangka, the island off the south-east coast of Sumatra, and
one of the tin islands of the Indies.

## Assistent-Residentie Billiton

The assistant-residency of Billiton, the island east of Bangka, known for its
tin and its pepper. Britain held it from 1812 and ceded it to the Netherlands
under the Anglo-Dutch treaty of 1824.

## Bengkoelen

Also Bencoolen and today Bengkulu. A residency on the west coast of southern
Sumatra. It had been a British colony, ceded to the Netherlands under the
Anglo-Dutch treaty of 1824.

## Lampongsche Districten

The division of the southern tip of Sumatra, across the Sunda strait from
Java, the country of the Lampung people.

## Sumatra's Westkust

The residency of the west coast of Sumatra, taking in the Mentawai, Nias,
Banyak and Batu islands.

## Sintang

A division of western Borneo on the upper Kapuas, the seat of a Malay
sultanate that turned Muslim in 1672.

## Bandjermasin

A division of southern Borneo, seat of the Banjar sultanate until the colonial
government dissolved it in 1860, and from 1938 the seat of the Borneo
gouvernement.

## Zuidoostkust van Borneo

A division of the south-east coast of Borneo, facing the Makassar strait,
called the afdeeling Pasir en de Tanah Boemboelanden until 1930.

## Doesoenlanden

A division of south-eastern Borneo, the inland country of the Dusun people.

## Pontianak

A division of western Borneo at the mouth of the Kapuas, seat of a Malay
sultanate founded in 1771 and of the Dutch residency for the west of the
island.

## Singkawang

A division of north-western Borneo, the country of the Malay sultanate of
Sambas and of the Chinese settlements at Montrado that the Dutch put down in
1854.

## Boeloengan

A division of the north-eastern corner of Dutch Borneo, the sultanate of
Bulungan, against the British North Borneo frontier.

## Samarinda

A division of eastern Borneo on the Mahakam, the country of the sultanate of
Kutai, whose court had sat at Tenggarong since 1782.

## Hoeloe Soengei

A division of south-eastern Borneo, the upper river country inland from
Bandjermasin.

## Koealakapoeas

A division of southern Borneo on the rivers west of Bandjermasin.

## Ketapang

A division of south-western Borneo facing the Java Sea, the old country of the
Tanjungpura kingdom.

## Bali en Lombok

The residency of Bali and Lombok, its seat at Singaradja on the north coast of
Bali. From 1938 it was one of the residencies of the Groote Oost gouvernement
at Makassar.

## Timor en Onderhoorigheden

The residency of the eastern Lesser Sundas, separated from the gouvernement of
the Moluccas in 1819, with its capital at Koepang on Timor.

## Celebes en Onderhoorigheden

The residency of southern Celebes, its seat Makassar, which from 1938 was also
the seat of the Groote Oost gouvernement.

## Manado

The residency of the northern arm of Celebes, seated at Menado. Its Minahasa
country was largely Christian in a mostly Muslim colony.

## Gouvernement der Molukken

The gouvernement of the Moluccas from 1925, which took in Dutch New Guinea as
well, seated at Ambon. In 1935 it became the residency of the Molukken, and
from 1938 part of the Groote Oost.

## West-Java

One of the three provinces Java and Madoera were divided into in 1925, its
capital Batavia, made up of the residencies of Bantam, Batavia, Buitenzorg,
Priangan and Cheribon.

## Midden-Java

One of the three provinces of Java from 1925, its capital Semarang, made up of
the residencies of Pekalongan, Semarang, Japara-Rembang, Banjoemas and Kedoe,
with the two Vorstenlanden beside it.

## Oost-Java

One of the three provinces of Java from 1925, its capital Soerabaja, covering
the east of the island and Madoera.

## Westerafdeeling van Borneo

One of the two gewesten of Dutch Borneo from 1848, when the old gouvernement
of Borneo en Onderhoorigheden was wound up. Its seat was Pontianak.

## Zuider- en Oosterafdeeling van Borneo

The other of the two gewesten of Dutch Borneo, covering the south and east of
the island, with its seat at Bandjermasin.

## Djapara-Rembang

The residency of the north-east coast of Central Java, seated at Pati, with
the regencies of Pati, Japara, Rembang, Blora and Koedoes.

## Besoeki

The residency of the eastern corner of Java, made by joining Bondowoso and
Djember.

## Madoera

The residency of the island of Madoera, seated at Pamekasan, with the Kangean
and Sapudi islands under it and four afdeelingen: Pamekasan, Bangkalan,
Sampang and Soemenep.

## Priangan

The residency of the Parahyangan highlands of West Java, seated at Bandoeng.
The Preanger Regencies had been split into three in 1925 and were put back
together under this name after 1931.

## Banjoemas

The residency of south-western Central Java, seated at Banjoemas, with the
regencies of Banjoemas, Poerwokerto, Tjilatjap, Karanganjar and Bandjarnegara.

## Gouvernement Borneo

From 1938 the Outer Possessions were put into three gouvernementen — Sumatra
at Medan, Borneo at Bandjermasin, and the Groote Oost at Makassar — and the
two Borneo residencies came under one governor.
