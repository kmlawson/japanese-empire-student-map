## Funafuti

The lagoon and the airstrip the assault on Tarawa and Makin was mounted from
in November 1943. Marines landed on 2 October 1942 and the islanders of the
main islet were moved to make room for the runway.

> Which cluster a sub-unit belongs to, where that changed between the two
> dates. The cluster is written into the SVG, and the SVG serves both. The
> Dindings — Lumut, Sitiawan and Pangkor — were a Straits Settlement from 1826
> and were retroceded to Perak on 16 February 1935, so on the December 1942
> map they are part of Perak: hovering them should not light Singapore, Penang
> and Malacca, and hovering Singapore should not light them. The key is the
> atom and the sub-unit's own name; the value is the cluster it belongs to on
> that date, or null for none.
