## e1930

Before the Manchurian Incident. Japan holds Taiwan, Korea, Karafuto, the
Kwantung leasehold and the South Seas Mandate. Everything else in the region
belongs to somebody else’s empire, and the colours here show whose.

## e1942

Ten years of expansion in China and a year of conquest in Southeast Asia and
the Pacific. The naval perimeter is at its widest a few months behind this
date and the tide has just turned: the fighting on Guadalcanal is in its fifth
month and the army will be withdrawn in February. The largest area of China
under Japanese control is still two years away.

> The map opens on 1930 and moves forward. Starting on 1942 showed the answer
> before the question: a student who has never seen the empire small has
> nothing to measure the large one against.
