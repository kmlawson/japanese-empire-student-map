## ccp

> Marker categories, shared by both epochs.

## pacified

The so-called "pacified areas" represented areas designated by the Japanese
military as relatively secure. This did not mean there was no resistance
activity within its area.

## unpacified

The so-called "un-pacified areas" represented areas designated by the Japanese
military as entirely unsecure. In practice, they often overlap with the
location of resistance base areas. The unmarked terrain between "pacified" and
"un-pacified" was marked "semi-pacified" and likely indicates regions where
both the Japanese and resistance forces contended for control or in some cases
left to their own devices.
