# Filling the gaps: Taiwan sub-units — 16 September 2026

Region `taiwan-fill`. Second pass; the audit is
`reports/2026.09.16-cards-korea-taiwan-manchuria.md`. Scope:

* `texts/territories/sub-units/taiwan.csv` / `.md` — 64 rows, 41 of them with
  no note.
* `texts/territories/sub-units/senkaku.csv` — 4 rows, none with a `short`.

Neither file is shared with another region, so there is no changes JSON. No
build was run.

Everything was fetched with `action=raw` from `ja.wikipedia.org` or
`zh.wikipedia.org` under the User-Agent `interactive-japan-map/1.0 (link
check)`; no personal information was sent. The `郡` articles are thin and almost
wholly administrative — which towns and villages a district held, where its
office was, what the ground is called now — so that is what most of these notes
say, with a particular added wherever the article gives one. Where a fact could
not be sourced it was left out rather than guessed; in particular **no Japanese
reading of a sub-district town was invented**. Towns whose Japanese reading the
sources give (the district seats, Matsuyama, Kiyomizu, Gosei, Nirin, Madō,
Kari, Makō, Bōan) are named in Japanese; everything below district level that
the sources give only in characters is named by its present-day form, which is
what the articles state.

Every new note opens `In <prefecture>-shū (漢字).`, following the two district
notes the file already had (`TwToen`, `TwHozan`) — the card shows the note
instead of the `short`, so it has to place itself.
