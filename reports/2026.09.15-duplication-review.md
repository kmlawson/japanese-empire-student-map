# Duplicate functions and inefficiencies, reviewed

**Date:** 15 September 2026, against the working tree at `c46edd8` (update 203).
**Subject:** duplicate and near-duplicate code, and run-time inefficiencies, in
the five hand-written root scripts (`map.js`, `trains.js`, `annotate.js`,
`admin.js`, `air-play.js`), `deploy/styles.css`, and the Python build tools.

## Method

Read in this order, and measured with scripts rather than eyeballed:

1. `reports/2026.09.09-js-and-tests-review.md`, to separate what is already
   known and acted on from what is new.
2. Every top-level function in `map.js` listed with its line and length, then
   grouped by name shape (`build*Menu`, `place*Menu`, `open*Menu`,
   `close*Menu`, `*Card`, box arithmetic) and the members of each family
   diffed against one another.
3. Cross-file: every helper defined in `trains.js`, `annotate.js`,
   `air-play.js` and `admin.js` checked against `map.js`'s own, and against
   what each module is handed by its host object.
4. Python: the four train builders and the station builders diffed pairwise.
5. Run-time: `querySelector*`, `getComputedStyle`, `getBBox`,
   `getBoundingClientRect` and `forEach` occurrences attributed to their
   enclosing function, and the enclosing function classified by how often it
   runs (per frame, per pointer move, per state change, once).
6. CSS: rule blocks normalised and hashed to find exact duplicates; selector
   lists counted for hand-extended enumerations.

Nothing in the repository was modified except this file. Another session was
editing `trains.js` and `deploy/index.html` while this ran; the hunks are all
below `trains.js:1227` and do not move any line cited here.

## 1 — duplicate families inside `map.js`

### 1.1 The six pick-menus: five near-identical copies of four functions

`#air-menu`, `#rail-menu`, `#theme-menu`, `#station-menu`, `#train-menu` are
built, placed, opened and closed by five parallel sets of functions; the
label menu (`#label-menu`, built in HTML) is a sixth member with two of the
four.

| | make the node | build rows | place | open | close |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| air | `airMenuEl` 16409 | `buildAirMenu` 16422 | `placeAirMenu` 16517 | `openAirMenu` 17004 | `closeAirMenu` 17013 |
| rail | `railMenuEl` 16550 | `buildRailMenu` 16563 | `placeRailMenu` 16640 | `openRailMenu` 16988 | `closeRailMenu` 16997 |
| theme | `themeMenuNode` 16663 | `buildThemeMenu` 16676 | `placeThemeMenu` 16732 | `openThemeMenu` 16744 | `closeThemeMenu` 16753 |
| station | `stationMenuEl` 16800 | `buildStationMenu` 16836 | `placeStationMenu` 16877 | `openStationMenu` 16889 | `closeStationMenu` 16898 |
| train | `trainMenuEl` 16912 | `buildTrainMenu` 16925 | `placeTrainMenu` 16960 | `openTrainMenu` 16972 | `closeTrainMenu` 16981 |
| label | (in `index.html`) | `buildLabelRows` 16297 | `placeLabelMenu` 16385 | `openLabelMenu` 17020 | `closeLabelMenu` 17030 |

Measured by normalising each body (comments stripped, ids and the `*MenuOn`
flag substituted):

* **`place*Menu` — five copies, byte-identical.** All five reduce to exactly
  the same 11 statements; only `'#air-menu'`/`'#btn-air'` and their four
  counterparts differ. `placeLabelMenu:16385` is the one real variant: it
  hangs *under* the button rather than to its left, because its button is in
  the bar and not beside the map.
* **`*MenuEl`/`themeMenuNode` — five copies, differing only in `m.id` and the
  `aria-label` string.** Ten lines each.
* **`close*Menu` — six copies, identical** (`closeLabelMenu` differs only in
  naming its local `menu` instead of `m`).
* **`open*Menu` — six copies of six statements.** The only variation is
  whether `build*` takes an argument (`openThemeMenu(ids)`,
  `openTrainMenu(cands)`) and that `openLabelMenu` calls `syncLabelBoxes()`
  instead of a builder because its rows are in the HTML.
* **`build*Menu` — four of the five share the row idiom**: `m.innerHTML = ''`,
  a `p.menu-head` with a title, then per row a `label.row` holding an
  `input` (checkbox or radio) and a `span.menu-text` optionally containing a
  `span.src` with the citation in brackets. `buildRailMenu:16585-16624`,
  `buildThemeMenu:16692-16724` and `buildTrainMenu:16931-16956` are the same
  twenty lines three times; `buildAirMenu`'s `addSetRow:16464` is the same
  again with a plain text node instead of the span — and the comment at
  `map.js:16684-16691` records that *this difference was a bug reported
  twice* (three flex children laid the name and the source out as two
  columns). One helper would have made the bug impossible.

**The replacement.** A single descriptor table plus four functions:

```js

function menuRow(m, opts)         // {type:'checkbox'|'radio', name, data, checked, text, src, title, onchange}

function openMenu(key, arg)       // replaces the six open*Menu; calls closeOtherMenus(key)
```

Callers to change: `closeOtherMenus:16784` (six lines become a loop over the

`syncAirMenu:16506`, and the press/hold wiring in `wireControls`

### 1.3 The lon/lat box family, and what it costs per frame

Five functions and one flag do overlapping arithmetic on the view box:

| | line | what it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `viewLonLat` | 2342 | the frame as [w, s, e, n] — four `unproject` calls |
| `viewMeets(box)` | 2357 | `viewLonLat()` then an overlap test |
| `boxInView(b, v)` | 2437 | the same overlap test with `TRAIN_BOX_PAD`, taking `v` as an argument |
| `groundHere(want)` | 2387 | `viewMeets` per `STATION_SYS` key, smallest-area wins |
| `trainBoxAt(useOff)` | 2452 | centre `unproject`, then a per-`TRAIN_SYS` normalised-distance score |
| `trainCandidates` | 2524 | the *same* score expression as `trainBoxAt`, copied |

Two concrete duplications:

* **`boxInView` is `viewMeets` with a pad.** The comment at `map.js:2430-2436`
  says as much, and says a second `viewLonLat` "would replace it for the whole
  module, which is what happened once and hid every map button" — the
  duplication is documented as a hazard and kept anyway. One
  `meets(box, v, pad)` with `v` defaulted to a cached frame covers both.
* **The score expression is written twice**, `map.js:2506-2507` and
  `map.js:2551-2552`, character for character:
  `Math.max(Math.abs(c.lon - (b[0]+b[2])/2) / Math.max(0.1, (b[2]-b[0])/2), …)`.
  Extract `boxScore(c, b)`.

**And `viewLonLat` is recomputed many times a frame.** `rescale:6133` calls
`railFade:2259` on every frame of every pan, wheel and pinch; `railFade` calls
`syncMapButtons:2591`, which asks, in this order:

| call in `syncMapButtons` | `viewLonLat()` evaluations |
| --- | ---: |
| `themesHere()` 2605 | 1 per `THEMES_FOR` key (1 today) |
| `themeFollowsView()` 2618 | 1, when a theme is up |
| `railZone()` 2643 | up to 5 (one per `STATION_SYS` key with a `ground`) |
| `railUnderView()` 2643, only if `railZone()` was empty | up to 5 |
| `railUnderView()` 2698 (sugar button) | up to 5 |
| `railUnderView()` 2711 | up to 5 |
| `trainZone()` 2773 → `trainBoxAt(true)` | 1 `unproject` + up to 1 `viewLonLat` |

That is **16 to 21 `viewLonLat` calls — 64 to 84 `unproject` calls — on every
animation frame**, plus one `Object.keys(STATION_SYS)` allocation and one
closure per `groundHere`, and `railUnderView()` is evaluated three separate
times per frame for an answer that cannot change within the frame. Also in
`syncMapButtons`: `$('#row-train-tools')` at 2769 is an uncached
`querySelector` on every frame, three lines below a comment
(`map.js:2582-2584`) explaining that the six buttons are cached precisely
because "`querySelector` on a document of eight thousand nodes is not free".

**The fix is one line of caching, not a rewrite:** memoise `viewLonLat` on
`view.x/y/w/h` (or on a `viewGen` bumped by `applyView`), hoist
`railUnderView()` into a local in `syncMapButtons`, and add `#row-train-tools`
to the `btnElsFound` block. Guarded by `tools/test/run10.js` (multi-zoom
measurements) and the `transport` group.

* `el()` is not only duplicated, it is *missing* from the file that needs it
  most: `map.js` calls `document.createElement` **230 times**, and the

  Put `download: downloadText` on `annHost` and route `map.js:20012` through
  it, and the next file type that leaves the page inherits the decision
  instead of re-making it.

## 3 — the Python tools

### 3.1 The four train builders

`build_tw_trains.py` (895), `build_kr_trains.py` (1,267),
`build_kf_trains.py` (358) and `build_mn_trains.py` (1,318) already share
`rail_route.py` and `trains_split.py` — the hard parts. What is still copied:

* **`our_stations()`, four copies in two identical pairs.**
  `build_tw_trains.py:223` and `build_kr_trains.py:391` are the same five
  lines (strip the JS wrapper, drop trailing commas, `json.loads`);
  `build_kf_trains.py:130` and `build_mn_trains.py:477` are the same nine
  lines (one record to a line). The only difference across all four is the
  file name — `<sys>-stations.js` — which is derivable from the system key.
  One `read_stations(sys)` in a shared module, handling both layouts, replaces
  all four.
* **`grab(text, name)` — pulling `const NAME = <json>;` out of a source
  bundle — is identical in `build_tw_trains.py:195` and
  `build_kr_trains.py:339`**, and `build_kf_trains.py:118 read_consts` is the
  same job done for a list of names at once. `read_consts` is the better
  signature; the other two should call it.
* **`name_key`/`fold` — four variants of "reduce a station name to what two
  sources can share".** `build_tw_trains.py:189` and
  `build_kr_trains.py:333` differ by a single trailing `.lower()`;
  `build_mn_trains.py:341 fold` adds the strip of a trailing 著/發/着/発 and a
  bracketed suffix; `build_kf_trains.py:114 fold` is the character map alone.
  These are genuinely four rules and should stay four — but they should be
  four named rules in one module with one shared `VARIANTS` table, not four
  private tables that can drift. (`VARIANTS` and `_VARIANTS` are separately
  maintained in `build_kr_trains.py` and `build_mn_trains.py` today.)
* **`sub(old, new, what)` — the asserted HTML substitution** — is written
  twice, `build_kf_trains.py:164` and `build_mn_trains.py:617`, with the same
  docstring argument above each `dress_html` ("Every substitution is asserted,
  so a source that has moved on fails the build instead of quietly shipping a
  page missing half of what was asked for"). The mn copy is the better one: it
  takes a `count`. The `RD_CSS`/`RD_BOX`/`RD_JS` readings furniture that both
  splice in is the same feature written twice as well.
* **The stats block at the foot of each `main()`** is the same five facts —
  trains, lines, stations, matched/placed, stretches — formatted four
  different ways (`build_kf_trains.py` tail, `build_mn_trains.py` tail,
  and the two `WARNING:` blocks under `if __name__` in tw and kr, which are
  **character-for-character identical across those two files**, eight lines
  each). A `report(sys, counts, warnings)` would make the four builds
  comparable to read, which they are not today.

**Suggested `tools/trains_lib.py`**: `read_stations(sys)`,
`read_consts(path, names)`, `fold(s, *, drop_suffix=False, lower=False)` over
one `VARIANTS`, `patcher(html, who)` returning the asserted `sub`, and
`report(...)`. Roughly 150 lines out of the four builders, and — more to the
point — one place for the readings furniture that three timetable pages now
carry.

## 4 — inefficiencies at run time

The 9 September review's layout-read caching held: `containerSize:5015` and
`uiBoxes:6200` are memoised, and of the 41 functions in `map.js` that force
layout, 38 do it once. `onPointerMove:7384`, `onHover:9240`,
`onPointerDown:7181`, `onPointerUp:7521`, `redrawHighlight:10017`,
`setHot:8417` and `placeLabels:6829` contain **no** `querySelector`,
`getBoundingClientRect`, `getComputedStyle` or `getBBox` at all. The pointer
path is clean.

What is left is the **per-frame** path, `rescale:6133 → railFade:2259`, and
the **per-state-change** path, `applyState:15215`.

### 4.1 `applyAir` — about 324 selector queries per frame

`applyAir:13202` is called from `railFade`, so on every frame of every pan,

Per frame it does:

| line | what | per frame |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 13251-13272 | walk `airGroup.children`, and for each a `set(sel, v)` closure that does `g.querySelector(sel)` for `.air-halo`, `.air-line`, `.air-line-shared`, `.air-line-idle`, `.air-hit` | 54 × 5 = **270 `querySelector`** |

The last one is the plain duplicate: the loop at 13251 has already walked
`airGroup.children` and read each `g`'s `data-air`, so the second loop is
looking up elements it was holding thirty lines earlier. Merging the two, and
hoisting the five `querySelector`s to a per-route record built once in
`buildAir:11567`, removes ~324 selector queries a frame. The `d` comparison
should be against a cached string on the record rather than `getAttribute`.

### 4.2 `syncMapButtons` — `viewLonLat` sixteen to twenty-one times a frame

Set out in §1.3 above. `railUnderView()` alone is called three times
(`map.js:2643, 2698, 2711`) for an answer that cannot change within one call,
and `$('#row-train-tools')` at `map.js:2769` is an uncached `querySelector`
three lines from the comment explaining why the other six are cached.
`railSwitches():2816` rebuilds an `Object.keys().map().concat()` on each of
its two calls per frame, for a list derived entirely from module constants.

### 4.3 `railFadeOne` — a `querySelectorAll` and up to 1,977 style writes a frame

`railFadeOne:2858` ends with

```js
$$('path.rail-tie', group).forEach(function (el) { el.style.opacity = String(t); });
```

`railFade` calls it seven times a frame, once per rail group. Six of the
groups are small (the built SVG carries 49 rail paths in total, 41 of them
Manchuria's), but **`#jp-rail` is built at run time from `deploy/jp-rails.js`,
which holds 1,977 lines** — and `applyState` clones a `.rail-tie` and a
`.rail-hit` beside each, so that group is 5,931 paths. With Japan's railways
on, every frame does a `querySelectorAll` over 5,931 nodes, allocates a
1,977-element array (`$$` is `slice.call`), and writes 1,977 inline
`opacity` values — all to the same number, which changes only when `view.w`
crosses the tie band. The sugar sheet adds another 531 paths on the same path.

`t` is a pure function of `view.w`. Cache the node list on the group
(invalidated when `applyState` rebuilds it) and skip the whole loop when `t`
has not changed since the last frame. This is the single largest per-frame win
available.

### 4.4 `applyState` — `getComputedStyle` 3,954 times, and ~210 document scans

Two separate problems in one function.

**(a) `railInk`/`railGround` in a 1,977-iteration loop.**
`applyState:15491` runs `$$('path.rail', g).forEach(…)` over each rail group
and, per path, calls `railInk(over)` (15503) and `railGround(over)` (15510).
`railInk:3790` → `fillLum:3777` → `getComputedStyle(atom).fill`, and
`railGround:3760` → `getComputedStyle(atom).fill`. For Japan's group `over` is
the string `'japan'` on **all 1,977 paths**, so this is 3,954
`getComputedStyle` calls on the same element for the same answer. `atomEls`
already caches the element; nothing caches the resolved fill. A
`Map` keyed on `over`, cleared at the top of `applyState`, reduces it to two
calls. (`fillLum` also re-runs its regex and the sRGB→linear conversion each
time.)

**(b) `querySelectorAll` inside `territories().forEach`.**
`applyState` iterates the territory list — 64 records for 1930, 71 for 1942 —
three times, and the third loop (`map.js:15347-15388`) runs, *for every
territory whether or not it can be hidden*:

* `$$('[data-edge-for="…"]', svg)` — 15361
* `$$('#sub-outlines [data-id="…"]', svg)` — 15368
* `$$('[data-id="…"]', hatchGroup)` — 15372

plus a fourth at 15335 in the `srcOnly` loop. That is **140-210 full-subtree
attribute-selector scans per `applyState`**, over a document that is 1,324
paths at load and well over 8,000 once the admin sheet, the fine coastlines
and `jp-rails.js` are grafted in. `applyState` has **53 call sites**.

The fix is the one the file already uses four lines below: `atomsOf`,
`atomHits`, `backingEls`, `backingEdges` and `seamEls` are all
built-once index maps. `edgeFor`, `outlineFor` and `hatchFor` should be three
more, built where the elements are created or grafted.

### 4.5 Smaller, same kind

* `applyView:5923` — `$('#zoom-reset')` on every frame. Cache it beside
  `btnStaEl`.
* `trainCandidates:2524` — `$$('path.rail', g)` then `el.getBBox()` per path
  for each system whose railway is on. With Japan on that is 1,977 `getBBox`
  calls, each a forced layout if the document is dirty. It runs on a press of
  the train button (`map.js:20305`), not per frame, so it is a stutter rather
  than a cost per frame — but it can be answered from the group's own
  `getBBox()` in one call for the common case.
* `moveD:1694` builds path data with `out +=` per point and a
  `Math.round(v*100)/100` per coordinate; it is called from
  `reprojectDocument:1816` over every path in the document on a projection
  change. Acceptable as a once-per-change cost; worth knowing it is O(points)
  with two rounds and two string builds each.
* `airSetOn:12531` is a linear scan over `airSets()` for every call; it is
  called once per route in `applyAir` (twice, given §4.1) — i.e. ~108 scans of
  22 entries a frame. `airSets()` should also return a keyed map.

### 4.6 How often "per frame" actually is

`railFade()` is called **directly from `applyView:5939`**, and `applyView` is
called three times inside `onPointerMove:7384` (at 7419, 7455 and 7489). It is
*not* behind the `requestAnimationFrame` gate below it — only `rescale()` is,
and `rescale()` calls `railFade()` again at 6152. So everything in §4.1-§4.3
runs **once per pointer-move event during a pan**, which on a 120 Hz trackpad
is more often than once per frame, and again on each zoom frame.

The full per-move cost of `railFade`, with the airlines and Japan's railways
on, is therefore roughly:

| | per pointer-move |
| --- | ---: |
| `applyAir` selector queries (§4.1) | ~324 |
| `syncMapButtons` `unproject` calls (§4.2) | 64-84 |
| `railFadeOne` style writes over `#jp-rail` (§4.3) | 1,977 |
| `syncLayerInfo:13023` — `rows.forEach` over 68 `LAYER_INFO` records with an `indexOf` each, plus an uncached `$('#btn-layer-info')` | 68 + 1 |

None of this is measured as milliseconds here; `tools/compare_perf.js
--admin` on a pan sweep is what would turn it into a number, and it should be
run before and after any of these changes.

## 5 — the stylesheet

`deploy/styles.css` is 4,929 lines, 659 rules, 418 distinct class and id
selectors.

**Unused rules: essentially none.** Every class and id in the file appears in
a script, in `index.html`, in an SVG or in a build tool, with one exception:
`.ccp-fill` (`styles.css:3052`, inside `#jmap.mono .hatch-fill,
#jmap.mono .chinabase, #jmap.mono .ccp-fill { display: none; }`) is written
nowhere. (`.plane-e1930`/`.plane-e1942` are built as `'plane-' + kind` at
`air-play.js:412`, and `.f-sea`/`.f-land` as `'flabel f-' + physical` at
`map.js:4572`; both are in use.) This is a clean stylesheet and worth saying.

**Duplicated declaration blocks: sixteen**, and most are colour mixes that
happen to coincide rather than rules that should be shared. Two are worth
naming: `.trains-head` (3522) and `.pop-head` (3748) are the same six
declarations — the small-caps card heading — and should be one class;
`#options .mono-row` (3128) and `.train-row` (3293) are both
`display:flex; align-items:center; gap:8px`.

**The hand-extended selector lists are the real finding**, and the file says
so itself. `styles.css:4199-4217` is a twenty-line comment recording that
writing `#rail-menu, #theme-menu[hidden]` instead of `#rail-menu[hidden],
#theme-menu[hidden]` produced three reported faults, and concluding "Every id
is written out in full in every list now. It is longer and it is the only form
that says what it means."

The trouble is that `map.js` **already sets a shared class on five of the six
menus** — `m.className = 'pick-menu'` at `map.js:16414, 16555, 16668, 16805,
16917` — and **`.pick-menu` appears nowhere in `styles.css`**. The comment at
`map.js:16659-16661` knows: "the class these three share is styled by nothing
and that trap has been sprung once already." So the enumeration is maintained
by hand seven times over (styles.css:4219, 4237, 4244, 4271, 4284, 4290, 4295
— 38 selector lines) for want of adding `class="pick-menu"` to the one menu
that lacks it, `#label-menu` at `deploy/index.html:371`.

**The railway list is the same shape, spread over four files.** The same set
of systems is enumerated by hand in

* `styles.css:1244-1254` — `#tw-rail.picked, #kr-rail.picked, #kf-rail.picked,
  #mn-rail.picked` (four)
* `railSysOf:13635` — `target.closest('#tw-rail, #kr-rail, #kf-rail, #mn-rail,
  #jp-rail')` (five)
* `setRailPicked:15007` — `Object.keys(STATION_SYS)` (five: tw, kr, kf, mn, jp)
* `RAIL_INFO:13673` and `RAIL_SWITCH_ROWS:13646` (six, with `burma`)

and the four do not agree. Traced through: **Burma's railway cannot be
pressed.** `applyState:15468` gives `burmaRailGroup` its `.rail-hit` bands like
every other network, but `railSysOf` does not list `#burma-rail`, so the press
falls through to the ground underneath and `RAIL_INFO.burma` — a note written
for a card — is reachable only from the menu row and the layer panel.

`#jp-rail.picked` matching nothing is currently harmless, but only by
accident: `showRailCard('jp')` is reached at `map.js:8171` when
`jpLineCard(target)` returns null, which needs a line with no name, and all
1,977 records in `jp-rails.js` have one. Add a nameless line and Japan's
network stops lighting, with nothing to say why.

A class on each group (`rail-net`, set where the group is built or grafted)
with a single `.rail-net.picked .rail` rule, and `railSysOf` closing on
`.rail-net` rather than on five ids, collapses all four lists into one and
makes the seventh network automatic.
