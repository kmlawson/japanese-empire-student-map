## Netherlands Indies: what a source has to be

Per the author's instruction of this session: the 1930s residencies and
gewesten do not map onto today's regencies and provinces, so a modern-unit
article is not a source for the residency. A DEI note is written only from
sources describing the unit of the time (nl.wikipedia on the residentie or
gewest, the *Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch-Indië*, the 1930 census); where
there is no such source the note is held to facts true regardless of boundary
— which island and coast, what the region lived on — and the row is marked
"general facts only" in the table below. Existing `dei.csv` wiki links are left
exactly as they are and no new one is added unless the article is the 1930s
unit itself.

### One thing to reconcile

`dei.md`'s existing `>` commentary says Java "had three gouvernements from
1930 — West Java from 1926, East Java from 1929". The nl source above dates
the division to the reorganisation of **1925** and calls the three units
*provincies*, with Jogjakarta and Soerakarta the two *gouvernementen* beside
them. Both are defensible — 1925 is the ordinance, 1926/1929/1930 are the
dates each came into force — and the notes say 1925. The commentary is the
author's and is not shown to the reader, so it is left alone.
