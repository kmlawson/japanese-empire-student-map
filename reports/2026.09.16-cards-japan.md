# Info-card audit — Japan (home islands, island groups) and Karafuto

16 September 2026. Scope: `texts/territories/sub-units/japan.csv|.md`,
`japan-islands`, `kuriles`, `ryukyus`, `bonins`, `senkaku`; the `Chichijima`,
`Hahajima` and `Iwo Jima (Iō-tō)` entries in `overrides-1942.md`; the
territory records `japan`, `ryukyu`, `ogasawara`, `chishima`, `karafuto` in
1930/1942; 66 city-names records and 29 sites records.

Sub-unit files were edited in place. Everything in a shared file
(`texts/territories/1930.md`, `1942.md`, `sites/*`, `city-names.*`,
`sub-units/overrides-1942.md`) is written to
`reports/2026.09.16-cards-japan.changes.json` instead.

## The mechanism, checked against map.js

Matches the brief. `splitGloss` (map.js:680) cuts `Name — gloss` on the
spaced em dash only; `shortOf` (:984) returns `r.short`, else the gloss when it
is ≤ 88 characters (`SHORT_MAX`); `hoverShort` (:967) strips a trailing `pop`
string, cuts the rest with `briefOf` (`BRIEF_MAX` 120, :945) and re-appends the
figures; the card (:10533) shows `head.note || split.gloss || shortOf(head)`.
One detail the brief does not mention: a `short` of 89–120 characters is shown
whole on the hover, because `briefOf` only cuts above 120 — so the 88-character
ceiling applies to glosses in `en`, not to `short`.

## Question 1 — hover vs card

No record in this scope carries a paragraph in `short` or in `en`. The longest
`short` is 88 characters (`japan-islands` / Teshima) and the longest gloss in
`en` is 62 (`ryukyus` / Haterumajima), so nothing had to be moved into a `.md`
note. Eleven `short` values run 81–88 characters; they are phrases, not
paragraphs, and were left alone except where question 3 applied.

Records whose `short` was a truncation of the note's opening clause and read as
a dangling fragment are listed under question 3.

Shorts rewritten (all in `japan.csv`, all because the `short` was the note's
opening clause cut mid-list and read as a fragment):

note behind it, so the gloss serves only the hover.

## Question 3 — style

The prose in this scope is the author's and reads as it should: concrete,
past tense, no stacked triads, no "testament to", no summarising flourishes.
Nothing was rewritten for style alone. The seven `short` fields above were the
only fragments, and they are listed under question 1.
