# The review's actions, as they were taken

15 September 2026. The four investigations of the day
(`2026.09.15-structure-review.md`, `-duplication-review.md`, `-tests-review.md`,
`-pan-zoom-performance.md`) and the hand-read of the two central scripts
(`-core-scripts-review.md`) each end in a ranked list. This is the record of
which items were done, in the order they were done, with what each was
checked against. Written as the work went, so a cut-off session loses nothing.

## Done

1. **trains.js, three small things** (core-scripts 1–3): `rescaled(k)` writes
   each mark's transform from `livePos` instead of parsing the old transform
   with a regex on every zoom frame; the three reader-facing notes that still
   said a connection's "alignment is not yet sourced" now say it runs along
   the traced railway where the map has one (`CONN_NOTE`, `FERRY_NOTE`); the
   chip loop uses its own index. Checked: `krtrains` 42, `trains` 180.
2. **The section banners the test runner could not see** (structure 1, 2).
   `shaded relief`, `THEMATIC LAYERS` and `the six railways, one by one`
   rewritten to the form `MAP_SECTIONS` reads, and thirteen banners added
   where a section had grown over another subject: the relief, the graticule,
   reprojection, the station layers, the gazetteer, the site labels, the air
   tap path, the lit cluster, railways and cities as GeoJSON, the CSV button
   and the population tables, the cards, the other menus, the population
   switches. Comments only. The `MAP_SECTIONS` rules for them wait on the
   test-suite report.
3. **Dead code out** (structure 3): the retired rail flash and the branch in
   `railFadeOne` that fed it; `syncRailMenu`; the write-only `themeMenuEl`,
   `btnStationsSyss`, `popTableAt`; `LINK_DP` in annotate.js; the `.ccp-fill`
   rule. The never-called host and module API members are left: one line each,
   and a test may yet want them.
4. **One class for every railway group** (duplication 6): `rail-net` on the
   five groups the SVG build writes and on Japan's built at run time;
   `railSysOf`, `setRailPicked` and the two `.picked` rules use it. This is
   also the fix for Burma's railway, which had hit bands but was not in the
   pressed-set list and so could not be pressed. Checked: `keys` 34,
   `jprails` 43, `mntrains` 68, `burma` 45.
5. **`railInk`/`railGround` memoised per ground within one `applyState`**
   (duplication 2): 3,954 `getComputedStyle` calls a state change with Japan's
   railways on become as many as there are grounds.
6. **`syncMapButtons` asks less per frame** (duplication 4, performance 7):
   `viewLonLat` memoised against the view and projection mode (it was asked
   16–21 times a frame through `viewMeets`), `railUnderView()` once instead of
   three times, `#row-train-tools` and `#zoom-reset` held instead of queried,
   `railSwitches()` computed once. Checked: `keys`, `sugar`, `layerinfo`.
7. **The rail ties are written once per zoom width** (performance 1,
   duplication 1): `railFadeOne` keeps the tie node list on the group and
   writes the opacity only when the fade value changes. Measured by the
   performance review at 187 → 41 ms of busy time for a 40-step drag with
   Korea's and Japan's railways on.
8. **The train tools' `bounds()` memoised** on the mounted data and the
   connections switch (performance 4): 62 → 34 ms a drag with the tools up.
9. **`railFade` has a frame mode** (core-scripts 1, duplication 3):
   `applyView` and `rescale` call `railFade(true)`, which does the fade, the
   buttons and the one air class that depends on the zoom, and leaves the
   full `applyAir` (some 324 selector queries) and `syncLayerInfo` to state
   changes. Checked: `air` 150, `airplay` 82, `keys`, `layerinfo`.
10. **Six pick menus, one machine** (structure 6, duplication 5): `PICK_MENUS`
    and `pickNode`/`pickHead`/`pickPlace`/`pickOpen`/`pickClose`, one open-menu
    key in place of six booleans, one outside-click closer, one Escape, one
    resize; the stylesheet's seven hand-kept six-id selector lists are seven
    `.pick-menu` rules and the label menu carries the class. map.js is 170
    lines shorter. **The first draft named its helpers `openMenu` and
    `closeMenu`, which the right-click menu already owned** — the CLAUDE.md
    trap, sprung a second time the same day and caught by `trains`.
11. **A build guard against duplicate function names**: `build_texts.py`
    refuses to build when a hand-written script declares the same top-level
    function twice. Proved on a planted duplicate.

12. **The four train builders share `tools/trains_lib.py`** (duplication 9):
    `read_stations`, `grab`, `read_consts` and an asserted `Patch`. All four
    rebuilt through it; every bundle and timetable page byte-identical.
13. **The runner** (tests 1, 2, 3, 4, 6): the job cap is eight and the
    default is all cores but two; `--dry` divides by the real job count; the
    six builders and three inert paths have trigger rules; the eighteen
    sections without a `MAP_SECTIONS` row have one; a `smoke` tier of 26
    scripts (657 checks, 36 s measured); and `all.js` starts its own static
    server on a free port when nothing answers on 8123, and stops it. `beta`
    had three hard-coded 8123s and now follows the served port.
14. **Sleeps converted where the condition was plain** (tests 5): `until()`
    polls on a timer rather than animation frames; `citytap`'s seven-second

    short margin; the negative cases keep the old length as a timeout.

    collects them per page already; `report()` now ends with one check per
