# Burma by district and state, population by race-group, 1931

Census of India, 1931 Volume XI Burma Part I. Report, pp240-244. Appendix B,
"Burma Racial Map", the *Schedule for Racial Map* — the figures represented on
the racial map at the beginning of the Report, one block per district or state:
an *All Races* total, then the race-groups the map shows for that district,
each with a *Percentage of Total*. Read from the scan at PDF pages 262–266.

The terms for ethnic groups are those of the original census.

Three things the schedule itself says:

* "(Each percentage is given correct to the nearest decimal place and it
  sometimes happens that the sum of the percentages does not total 100.)"
* "Where the population of a race-group was too small to be shown on the map,
  its figures were included in the remainder group 'Others.'" So a race-group
  absent from a district's block is not zero — it is inside that district's
  Others. The smallest population shown on the map is about 600.
* A footnote "* Too small to be shown on the map" is attached to the Others
  row of Kyaukpyu and of Thatôn; Thatôn's Others prints "…" in the percentage
  column.

Spelling variants in the source, normalised in `fields.csv` to one label per
column: Myitkyina and the Southern Shan States print "Lolo-Muhso" where the
Northern Shan States print "Lolo-Muhso Group" (label used: Lolo-Muhso Group);
Kyauksè prints "Indo-Burma Races" where every other district prints
"Indo-Burman Races" (label used: Indo-Burman Races).

## Checks

Every district block was summed against its printed All Races total: 39 of 41
reconcile exactly; Minbu (−2) and the Southern Shan States (−300) do not, as
marked above. The 41 printed totals sum to 14,647,499. The schedule prints no
Burma total of its own, so no row for the province as a whole was invented.

## How the rows are keyed

37 of the 41 census units match a sub-unit key in
`texts/territories/sub-units/burma-admin.csv` (four of them under variant
spellings: Tharrawaddy → `Tharawaddy`, Thayetmyo → `Thayetmo`, Arakan Hill
Tracts → `Arakan Hill Distric`, and the diacritics of Pyapôn, Thatôn, Kyauksè,
Pakôkku, Yamèthin dropped). Karenni is keyed to `Karenni States`. Rangoon is
keyed to the city `rangoon` in `data/cities-1930.csv`, the census returning
the town as a district of its own. Insein and the two Shan States federations
have no shape of their own on this map and are carried as `unmapped`.
Kantarawadi, Kyebogyi and Bawlake carry the Karenni figure through `same_as`,
those three being the Karenni States of 1931 — an identification from the
literature, not from these pages, which name no constituent states.
