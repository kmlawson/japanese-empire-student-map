# 民籍または国籍および男女別人口 — Japan by race or nationality, 1940

**第5表 民籍または国籍および男女別人口（全人口）— 全国**, printed pp. 362–363,
from the same 1940 statistical yearbook as
`1940-Population-Japan-Prefectures.md` — scanned and hosted by the Program for
Constructing Data Infrastructure for the Humanities and Social Sciences,
Institute of Economic Research, Hitotsubashi University —
<https://d-infra.ier.hit-u.ac.jp/Japanese/govstat-database/statistical-yb/1940/>.

Whole country only; the table has no prefecture rows. Okinawa is included, and
the total agrees with the prefecture table's 73,114,308.

## What reaches the card, and what does not

All eighty-three rows are below. The card on the map carries the four 外地
registers whole, and of the seventy-five foreign nationalities it names every
one with **a hundred people or more** — twenty-four of them — keeps the source's
own その他 as a line of its own, and adds the remaining **fifty countries in one
line, 1,006 people between them**. Those fifty are named here and nowhere else:
a card of eighty lines, most of them a handful of people, is a list rather than
a fact.

ユダヤ人 is named on the card whatever its size — nine people — because it is a
category the census chose to count and not a small country.

Both blocks are kept out of the box's table (`table_skip`), which has
forty-eight rows and only one of them with a figure in these columns.

## A note on the source's English

The printed English is 1940's and is kept in the table below: *Inlanders*,
*Oversea's Inders*, *Saghalien*, *South Sea's Inders*, *Judaic*, and misspellings
like *Bragil*, *Chilo*, *Hiti*, *Newzealand*, *Venzuela*, *Esthonia* and
*Yugoslovia*. The map's cards say **Registered in Japan proper** and
**Registered in the 外地** instead, and give the countries their present names.

## What the figures say

- 朝鮮人 are 1,241,315 of the 1,265,049 registered in the 外地 — 98.1 per cent —
  and 1.7 per cent of everybody in Japan proper.
- The largest foreign nationality is 中華民国 at 19,453, half of all foreigners,
  and the next is the United States at 4,755.
- 白系露人, White Russians, are counted as a nationality of their own at 537:
  stateless since 1921 and listed among foreigners because there was nowhere
  else to put them.
