# 戸口靜態總表 — Taiwan Colony-wide Population, 1941 (昭和16年末)

Source: 臺灣總督府第四十五統計書 (昭和16年, publ. 昭和18年), Table 15 戸口靜態總表, pp. 18–19.
Population (人口) only; the 戸數 (household) columns of the source are omitted.

**Digit grouping:** the original prints numbers in Japanese 万 style, grouped in **fours**, not threes
— e.g. "36 7213" = 367,213 and "624 9468" = 6,249,468. All figures below have been converted to
standard 3-digit comma grouping.

Every row has been arithmetically verified: the ethnic/nationality components sum to the printed
total, male + female equals the total for every group, and the eight 州/廳 sum exactly to the
colony total in all twenty columns.

## Source misprints

Three cells in section 3 are internally inconsistent with the rest of their own row. Each is
reproduced above exactly as printed; the value implied by the other figures is given here. In every
case the surrounding figures agree with each other in two independent ways (male + female, and the
three components summing to the grand total), so the single printed cell is the outlier.

- **\* 1909 (明42) 本島人 female — printed 1,496,900.** The row's own female total (1,533,959) and the
  本島人 total (3,146,505) both require **1,496,800**. Verified by zoom: the page really does print 6900.
- **\*\* 1921 (大10) 外國人 total — printed 28,182.** Its own male + female (22,416 + 6,066) and the row
  total both require **28,482**. Verified by zoom.
- **\*\*\* 1928 (昭3) 內地人及朝鮮人 total — printed 211,702.** Its own male + female (113,660 + 97,542) and
  the row total both require **211,202**. Verified by zoom.

## Notes

- Original column headings: 總數 (總數/男/女), 內地人, 本島人, 朝鮮人, 中華民國人, 其ノ他ノ外國人 —
  each subdivided 總數/男/女. The pre-1932 table instead uses 內地人及朝鮮人, 本島人, 外國人.
- 內地人 = Japanese from the home islands; 本島人 = Taiwanese (Han and, in these totals, plains
  aborigines); the source's note records that up to 1935 the classification was 種族別 (by race)
  and from 1936 本籍・國籍別 (by domicile and nationality).
- The colony-wide table also prints per-thousand shares (總數千中) and sex ratios (女百ニ付男);
  these derived columns are not reproduced here.
- District-level (郡/市) figures for 1941 are in "1941 Taiwan Population by District.md".
