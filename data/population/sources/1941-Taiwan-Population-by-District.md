# 市街庄別常住戸口 — Taiwan Population by District (市 / 郡 / 支廳), 1941 (昭和16年末)

Source: 臺灣總督府第四十五統計書 (昭和16年), Table 16 市街庄別常住戸口, pp. 20–33.
All 64 second-tier divisions: 11 市 (cities), 51 郡 (districts) and 2 支廳 (Hōko sub-prefectures).
The 街 / 庄 / 蕃地 sub-rows of the source are **not** included, per request; nor are the 戸數
(household) columns — population (人口) only.

**Digit grouping:** the original prints numbers grouped in **fours** (Japanese 万 grouping), not
threes — e.g. "36 7213" = 367,213 and "16 6962" = 166,962. All figures below are converted to
standard 3-digit comma grouping.

"—" reproduces a "…" (none) entry in the source.

## Verification

Every one of the 64 rows satisfies all three internal identities:

1. 內地人 + 本島人 + 朝鮮人 + 中華民國人 + 其ノ他ノ外國人 = 總數
2. the same identity separately for the 男 columns and for the 女 columns
3. 男 + 女 = 總數 within every one of the six groups

Independently of that, the rows sum **exactly** to the eight prefecture totals printed in the
colony-wide table (see "1941 Taiwan Population Colony-wide.md"), column by column:

## Notes

- Original column headings: 總數 (總數/男/女), 內地人, 本島人, 朝鮮人, 中華民國人, 其ノ他ノ外國人 —
  each subdivided 總數/男/女. The 戸數 (household) block that precedes them is omitted here.
- 內地人 = Japanese from the home islands; 本島人 = Taiwanese.
- The 蕃地 (aboriginal territory) rows of the source are sub-rows of their 郡 and are included in
  the 郡 totals, but are not listed separately here.
- The name of 臺南州's 新豐郡 is badly ink-blotted in the scan. It is identified from its sub-rows
  (仁德庄, 關廟庄 — both 新豐郡 villages) and confirmed by elimination: it is the only one of
  臺南州's ten 郡 otherwise unaccounted for, and including it makes the prefecture sum exact.
- Scan quality on these pages is poor; every figure above was accepted only after passing the three
  internal identities and the prefecture-total reconciliation described above.
