# 人口ノ府縣分布 — Japan by prefecture, 1930, 1925 and 1920

**第四表 人口ノ府縣分布**, from 内閣統計局『昭和五年国勢調査最終報告書』p. 10 —
<https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1281995/1/30>.

Three censuses in one table: 昭和五年 (1 October 1930), 大正十四年 (1 October
1925) and 大正九年 (1 October 1920), for 内地 — Japan proper — one line to each
of the 1 道 3 府 43 縣. The three columns on the right are the source's own
人口千中, each prefecture's share of the whole per thousand, rounded to a whole
number; they are transcribed as printed and are not what the map divides by.

The table's own note reads: 備考　大正九年及大正十四年ノ人口ハ昭和五年調査當時ノ
市町村ノ境域ニ依リ組替ヘタルモノナリ — *the 1920 and 1925 populations have been
recast onto the municipal boundaries as they stood at the 1930 census*. So the
three columns are comparable with one another, which is the whole point of the
table.

**Checked against the source's own arithmetic.** The forty-seven prefectures
sum to the printed 全國 total in all three years, exactly:

## The areas

The report's fourth table has none, so `area_km2` in `japan-1930.csv` is
measured — `tools/measure_areas.py` over `tools/cache/adm1_JPN.json`, the same
file the map's prefecture outlines are drawn from, by spherical excess on a
sphere of 6371.0088 km. The measurement is close: Tottori 3,507 km² against a
received 3,507, Saitama 3,797 against 3,798, Nagano 13,552 against 13,562,
Iwate 15,263 against 15,275. The forty-seven come to 372,593 km².

Two departures are worth saying out loud.

**Hokkaidō is 78,290 km² here and 83,424 is the figure usually given.** The
difference is the Northern Territories — Etorofu, Kunashiri, Shikotan and the
Habomais, about 5,000 km² — which Japan counts in Hokkaidō and which *this map
draws as part of the Kurile Islands (Chishima)*, a territory of its own. The
area is therefore right for the shape a reader is pointing at, which is the
rule this folder follows. The population is the census's, and the census
counted Chishima's few thousand people in 北海道; against 2.8 million it does
not move the density.

**The island prefectures run a little under**, because the outlines are
simplified and the smallest islands are not in them: Nagasaki 4,066 km²
against 4,131 (−1.6%), Kagawa 1,862 against 1,877, Okinawa 2,265 against
2,281. Everything else is inside half a percent.
