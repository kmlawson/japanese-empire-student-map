**大正十四年 (1925)** and **大正九年 (1920)**.

Source: 内閣統計局『昭和五年国勢調査最終報告書』p. 25.
<https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1281995>

image was to hand, and all twenty-four of its 1930 figures match this reading

## What the column says

**Okinawa is the lowest in the country in all three years, and falling** —
89.53 men to 100 women in 1930 against a national 101.03. Kagoshima is next at
93.85. At the other end Tōkyō is 111.83 and Hokkaidō 109.28: the two the
country was moving *to*.
