# Taiwan local population, 1930 — 州/廳, 市, and 郡 transcription

Sources: `1930 Population Taiwan Local 1.pdf` through `1930 Population Taiwan Local 4.pdf`, scanned volume pp. 30–37.

Scope: the 州/廳 jurisdiction headings and the numeric 市 and 郡 rows requested by the user. 支廳, 區, 街, and 庄 entries are omitted. In table 37 the 州/廳 names are group headings rather than numeric rows; they are therefore preserved below as section headings. The later pages (table 38) were used to verify the 郡 jurisdiction names but their 街/庄 data are not transcribed. Numbers are copied as printed, with commas added as thousands separators.

Columns in every table: 總數, 內地人, 本島人, and 外國人, each divided into total, male, and female.

## Reading notes

- All seven 市 rows and all 45 numeric 郡 aggregates on p. 30 are included. Each 郡 aggregate was matched to its corresponding 郡 section on pp. 31–36. The later-page headings themselves carry no numeric aggregate cells; their numbers belong to the subordinate 街/庄 rows, which the user asked to omit.
- Several final digits were faint in the scan. The most difficult were 基隆郡 內地人總數, 宜蘭郡 外國人男/女, and 文山郡 總數. They were read as 1,566; 297/99; and 58,529 respectively after zoomed inspection and confirmation that male + female = total and that 內地人 + 本島人 + 外國人 = 總數.
- 員林郡 外國人女 is printed as `125` and is transcribed literally. The source row is internally inconsistent by one person: 外國人男 `405` + 外國人女 `125` = `530`, although the printed 外國人總數 is `531`; likewise the female category figures sum to `75,470`, while the printed female total is `75,471`. A tight visual crop favors `125`, so it is not silently emended to `126`.
- In a few other faint cells (notably 豐原郡 外國人), the arithmetic checks were used to distinguish the last digit.
- No numeric 州/廳 total row is printed in table 37. The headings are therefore recorded without invented or imported totals.
