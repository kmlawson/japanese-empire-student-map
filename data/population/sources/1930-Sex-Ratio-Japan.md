# 男女別人口 — males per 100 females, Japan 1930

From 内閣統計局『昭和五年国勢調査最終報告書』p. 25 —
<https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1281995/1/30>.

**This transcription is not complete.** The scan reaches 愛知 and stops; the
twenty-three prefectures from 三重 to 沖縄 are not here and their rows carry no
sex ratio on the map. A blank field is simply left off a card, so nothing looks
broken — but the column is half a table until the rest of the page arrives.

Japan proper stands at 101.03 men to a hundred women. The spread is what the
figure is for: 東京 111.83 and 神奈川 107.56 at one end and 長野 94.07, 石川
94.84 and 群馬 96.02 at the other — cities pulling men in and the silk and rice
country sending them away. 北海道 at 109.28 is the settlement frontier.
