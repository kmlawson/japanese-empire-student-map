Source: Table 40 (職業（大分類）別本業人口 府郡島), scan pp. 124–133. Province (道) and all-Korea rows only.
Columns: 1–9 = total gainfully occupied (有業者); categories 1 農業 Agriculture, 2 水產業 Fisheries,
3 鑛業 Mining, 4 工業 Industry, 5 商業 Commerce, 6 交通業 Transport, 7 公務自由業 Public service &
professions, 8 家事使用人 Domestic servants, 9 其ノ他ノ有業者 Other gainfully occupied, 10 無業 No occupation.
The "Both sexes" table is computed (male + female). Verified: categories 1–9 sum to the printed 1–9 total,
1–9 + no-occupation equals each region's total population (cross-checked against the census population
tables), and the 13 provinces sum to the 全鮮 row.
