# Sources for `report.md`

Research done in Japanese and Chinese. Grouped by what each source was used
for. Everything was consulted between 10 and 12 August 2026.

A note on reliability, since these sources do not all sit on the same footing:
the Japanese Wikipedia articles on operations and units are largely compiled
from the *Senshi Sōsho* official history and are reasonably dependable for
dates, unit designations and command appointments. The Chinese
resistance-commemoration sites (`krzzjn.com`, provincial party-history pages,
`nmgqq.com.cn`) are reliable for chronology and place names and unreliable for
casualty figures, which are consistently the wartime Nationalist or Communist
claims. `baike.baidu.com` was used only for cross-checking dates already found
elsewhere. The one peer-reviewed scholarly item — Futaki 2021 — carries more
weight than everything else here and is the basis for section 4 of the report.

## Scholarly

* Futaki Hiroshi 二木博史, 「蒙疆政権発行の行政区画地図 ―蒙疆政権研究のための基礎資料―」,
  *日本とモンゴル* 第55巻（141号）, March 2021, pp. 148–152.
  <https://www.mongol-kyokai.or.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/141_11_1.pdf>
  — The key source. Catalogues the five known Japanese-language administrative
  maps published by the Mengjiang regime and their holding institutions, and
  makes the argument that the regime's own maps drew controlled territory into
  Bayantala League, leaving "Ikh Juu League" as a label for what it did not
  control. Also cites Jinhai 金海, 『日本占领时期内蒙古历史研究』, Hohhot:
  内蒙古人民出版社, 2005, p. 75 for the June 1940 move of the Shilingol League
  office, and notes the classified Garrison Army terrain maps
  『内蒙古地方五十万分一地誌図』 (April 1939) and the Land Survey Department's
  『二百万分一蒙疆素図』 (April 1942).

* 成茹 (?), 「蒙古軍政府成立前後における関東軍と徳王による募兵工作」, Kobe University.
  <https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/0100482492/0100482492.pdf>
  — Kwantung Army and Demchugdongrub recruitment work around the founding of
  the Mongol Military Government. Consulted for the 1936 political background.

* 「蒙疆政権期，オラーンチャブ盟の設立とその意義」, *アジア経済* 65(4).
  <https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ajiakeizai/65/4/65_34/_html/-char/ja>
  — Establishment of Ulanchab League under Mengjiang.

* 内田知行, 「第6章 蒙疆政権のアヘン政策」.
  <https://spc.jst.go.jp/cad/literatures/download/11839>
  — Mengjiang's opium policy.
